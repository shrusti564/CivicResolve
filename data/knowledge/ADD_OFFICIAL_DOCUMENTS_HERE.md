# Add official documents here

Place verified civic procedures, circulars, grievance manuals, department contact lists and service-level documents in this folder as PDF, TXT or Markdown. Then click “Re-index knowledge base” on the admin dashboard or POST to `/api/rag/ingest`.

Do not use this folder for unverified social-media posts. Include source agency, publication date and URL in the document text so that retrieved citations can be checked.
